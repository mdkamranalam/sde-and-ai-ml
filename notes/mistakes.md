# Mistakes & Lessons

A record of mistakes made while learning, implementing, debugging, or designing systems.

The objective is not to record mistakes for their own sake.

The objective is to prevent repeating them.

---

# Entry Format

## YYYY-MM-DD — Topic

### Context

What was being implemented or studied?

### Mistake

What went wrong?

### Root Cause

Why did it happen?

### Fix

How was it resolved?

### Lesson

What principle should be remembered?

### Prevention

How can the mistake be avoided in the future?
